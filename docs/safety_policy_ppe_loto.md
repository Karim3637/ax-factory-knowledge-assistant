# EHS-POL-003: Personal Protective Equipment & Lockout/Tagout
(Fictional sample document created for demo purposes – not an official company document)

## 1. PPE Requirements by Area

Production floor (all areas): Safety shoes and high-visibility vest are mandatory at all times.

Welding area: Welding helmet with auto-darkening filter (shade 10–13), flame-resistant jacket, leather welding gloves, safety shoes. Hearing protection is required when noise exceeds 85 dB.

Painting / chemical area: Half-face respirator with organic vapor cartridges, chemical-resistant nitrile gloves, safety goggles, and disposable coverall.

Press and stamping area: Cut-resistant gloves (level 5), safety glasses, and hearing protection.

Warehouse: Safety shoes, vest, and a helmet when working under racking higher than 2 meters.

## 2. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure
LOTO must be applied before any maintenance, cleaning, or repair on equipment with hazardous energy.

Step 1 – Notify: Inform the machine operator and the area supervisor that maintenance will start.
Step 2 – Shut down: Stop the machine using the normal stop button.
Step 3 – Isolate: Switch off and isolate all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic).
Step 4 – Lock & tag: Apply your personal red lock and a tag with your name, date, and reason.
Step 5 – Release stored energy: Bleed hydraulic/pneumatic pressure, discharge capacitors.
Step 6 – Verify: Try to start the machine to confirm it is fully isolated (zero-energy check).
Step 7 – Restore: After work, remove tools, reinstall guards, remove your own lock only, and notify the operator.

Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Lost-key situations must be escalated to the EHS manager.

## 3. Incident Reporting
Any injury or near-miss must be reported to the shift supervisor immediately and logged in the EHS system within 24 hours.
EHS emergency extension: 2000.
