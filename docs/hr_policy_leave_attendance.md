# HR-POL-007: Leave & Attendance Policy
(Fictional sample document created for demo purposes – not an official company document)

## 1. Working Hours
Day shift: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Night shift: 8:00 PM – 4:00 AM.
A grace period of 10 minutes is allowed. Arriving more than 10 minutes late three times in one month results in a written warning.

## 2. Annual Leave
Employees with less than 1 year of service: 15 working days (available after completing 6 months).
Employees with 1–10 years of service: 21 working days.
Employees with more than 10 years of service or over 50 years old: 30 working days.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance and approved by the direct manager.

## 3. Sick Leave
Sick leave requires a medical certificate submitted within 48 hours. The first 3 days are paid at 100%.

## 4. Company Transportation
Company buses depart from Cairo (Nasr City, Heliopolis, Abbas El Akkad) and from Sharqia. Bus schedules are available in the HR portal. Employees must use their badge when boarding.

## 5. Contact
HR helpdesk: ext. 3100, or via the HR portal "Ask HR" form.
