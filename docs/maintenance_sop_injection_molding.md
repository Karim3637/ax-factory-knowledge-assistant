# SOP-MNT-012: Injection Molding Machine – Alarms & Preventive Maintenance
(Fictional sample document created for demo purposes – not an official company document)

## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to all injection molding machines in the plastics production line (Lines 1–4).

## 2. Common Alarm Codes

Alarm E-101 – Hydraulic oil temperature high (above 55°C).
Action: Check that the oil cooler water valve is open. Verify cooling water flow is at least 20 L/min. If temperature does not drop within 15 minutes, stop the machine and notify the maintenance supervisor.

Alarm E-104 – Mold clamp pressure low.
Action: 1) Stop the cycle and put the machine in manual mode. 2) Check the hydraulic oil level in the tank sight glass; refill with ISO VG 46 oil if below the minimum mark. 3) Inspect clamp cylinder hoses for leaks. 4) Reset the alarm. 5) If the alarm repeats more than twice in one shift, open a maintenance ticket in the CMMS with priority "High".

Alarm E-110 – Barrel heater zone failure.
Action: Check heater band continuity with a multimeter (expected 20–40 ohms). Replace the heater band if open-circuit. Only qualified electricians may perform this task, after applying Lockout/Tagout.

Alarm E-120 – Safety gate open.
Action: Close the operator gate fully. Never bypass the safety gate switch. Bypassing safety devices is a disciplinary offense.

## 3. Preventive Maintenance Schedule
Daily: Check hydraulic oil level, clean the mold area, check for oil leaks.
Weekly: Lubricate the toggle mechanism and tie bars, inspect the nozzle for wear.
Monthly: Replace the hydraulic return filter, calibrate barrel thermocouples.
Every 4,000 hours: Full hydraulic oil change and oil analysis.

## 4. Escalation
Shift maintenance technician -> Maintenance supervisor (ext. 2210) -> Plant engineering manager.
